# Repository A+ review contract

Based on DTO Brand A+ Guideline v1.0 (November 4, 2026), adapted explicitly to repository review. This is an acceptance standard, not an awarded grade or accessibility/legal certification.

Retain the same thresholds: at least **97.00/100 without rounding**, at least **90% in every category**, every one of **40 criteria at least 3/4**, all eight hard gates passed, complete dated evidence and no unresolved Critical/Major defect. Unknown or unrun is not passed. Do not drop difficult criteria or redistribute their weights.

## Declared surface mapping

| Category / weight | Five criteria and repository interpretation |
|---|---|
| I — Identity, 12 | I1 recognizable owner; I2 personal/other-project boundaries; I3 technical audience/value; I4 plain truthful voice; I5 consistent canonical identity/contact |
| V — Visual system, 18 | V1 original approved mark; V2 correct optical use; V3 sufficient color states; V4 readable text/fallbacks; V5 coherent README/report density |
| A — Accessibility, 20 | A1 headings/links/alternatives; A2 keyboard/focus; A3 contrast and non-color cues; A4 narrow/zoom/text access; A5 complete applicable owned-surface evidence, with GitHub-controlled limits disclosed |
| C — Content/evidence, 16 | C1 accurate claims; C2 problem/contribution/maturity; C3 exact source/run/date provenance; C4 incomplete evidence visible; C5 public suitability and rights |
| N — Navigation, 12 | N1 reviewer/developer journeys; N2 meaningful hierarchy; N3 working source/docs/license links; N4 compact current-change/status discovery (repository counterpart of the watch route); N5 useful missing/unavailable/failure states |
| X — Consistency, 8 | X1 correct accounts; X2 appropriate profile/repository adaptation; X3 canonical destinations; X4 matching source/package/report identity; X5 honest per-surface rollout |
| D — Delivery, 8 | D1 independent readback; D2 accurate metadata; D3 appropriate assets and package size; D4 reproducible CI/publication and false-success refusal; D5 editable maintainer handoff |
| G — Governance, 6 | G1 fixed authorized scope; G2 conflict-aware changes; G3 retained rollback; G4 complete criterion evidence/reviewer/date; G5 defensible scoped release decision |

Each criterion needs a 0–4 rating and concrete evidence at the final commit. This repository mapping does not modify or grade the website's original rubric.

## Mandatory gates

HG1 identity/authority; HG2 truth/provenance; HG3 privacy; HG4 accessibility; HG5 navigation/availability; HG6 asset integrity/rights; HG7 publication/automation behavior; HG8 release/recovery.

For HG7, inspect source-preserving migration, CI/artifact failure handling and source/readback identity instead of claiming an unrelated website feed was tested. For check-ins, HG3/HG6/HG7 additionally require disclosure, applicable licensing, receiver permissions and real delivery/deduplication evidence before enabling the feature.

## Current outcome

Full rendered public/keyboard/narrow-screen review, source/third-party ownership closure, historical audit, actual Windows 11 operator acceptance, receiver/client deployment and operational recovery remain separate. Do not display an A+ badge or infer complete product readiness from CI.
