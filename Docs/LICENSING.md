## Existing licenses that must not be overridden

The profile repository contains [GPLv3 text](https://github.com/davidtomofficial/davidtomofficial/blob/main/LICENSE). The Python, SQL and cpp-Examples READMEs also retain GPLv3 statements; Python's googleSearch file additionally states v3 or later. The profile's license does not automatically govern unrelated repositories, and absence of a local LICENSE file does not justify deleting an explicit grant already present in source or documentation.

Do not add a permission fee, mandatory tracking condition or no-modification restriction to an existing GPL grant. Prior authorized distributions retain their applicable rights. Any future dual-license or relicensing decision needs a verified rights chain and review of all contributors/dependencies; no such decision is made here.

## Distribution checklist

- Keep the complete permission notice in the root, component source packages and release documentation, with an accessible link.
- Preserve applicable third-party license/attribution files. Approved outlined DT artwork is not permission to redistribute font software or impersonate the owner.
- Show license/check-in conditions before enrolling an authorized user or system; record actual assent and applicable terms. Website visibility or a checkbox in a TODO is not assent.
- Obtain qualified review of any distribution/customer agreement, privacy notice and ownership uncertainties before operational distribution. This documentation does not guarantee enforceability or remove nonwaivable rights.
