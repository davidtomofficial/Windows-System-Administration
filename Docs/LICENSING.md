## GitHub and separate agreements

GitHub's [Terms of Service, section D.5](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service#5-license-grant-to-other-users) covers other users' rights to use, display, perform and reproduce public content by forking through the service as its functionality permits. Section D.6 addresses contributions under the repository's applicable license, subject to a separate agreement with different terms. These platform terms remain applicable; the rights notice does not override them or turn the repository into an unrestricted software-use grant.

The [agreement status record](AGREEMENT-STATUS.md) distinguishes the current notice from an adopted customer EULA, CLA, DCO requirement or copyright assignment. The [usage-check-in document](USAGE-CHECK-IN.md) is a draft technical specification. It does not establish an executed agreement, approved cadence or active enforcement.

## Distribution checklist

- Keep the complete permission notice in the root, component source packages and release documentation, with an accessible link.
- Preserve applicable third-party license/attribution files. Approved outlined DT artwork is not permission to redistribute font software or impersonate the owner.
- Show license/check-in conditions before enrolling an authorized user or system; record actual assent and applicable terms. Website visibility or a checkbox in a TODO is not assent.
- Obtain qualified review of any distribution/customer agreement, privacy notice and ownership uncertainties before operational distribution. This documentation does not guarantee enforceability or remove nonwaivable rights.
